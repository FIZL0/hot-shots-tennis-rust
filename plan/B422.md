# B422 — Roster categories you can see (concept, user 2026-10-15)

Concept approved by the user 2026-10-15 (mockup canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/NbYnjG3uiGDpnH9AXEQSi6).

## Problem
B382's one scrolling grid hides everything but the section under the cursor. In a 2-player column only ~2 tile rows
fit: Favorites scrolls off the top, the mod games sit below the fold with nothing hinting at them, and the footer
"Hot Shots Tennis · 8 of 16" reads as the whole cast. Players think there are no other characters.

## Concept: a category rail + "more below"
(Missing mod tiles under a visible heading, user 2026-10-15 phone shot, is its own bug: B426, before these.)
1. **Category rail** (B422) — one slim row of chips above each column's grid, always visible, never scrolls:
   `★ 2 · Hot Shots Tennis 16 · Get a Grip 2 · Golf 7 · Mods 3`. The cursor's section is filled in the seat's
   colour, the rest outlined. Chips keep their own width; if they don't fit, the rail scrolls sideways to keep the
   current chip in view with a fade + "›" at the cut edge (unlike B391's squished tabs: never shrink the text).
   L2/R2 and a tap on a chip still jump sections (same `Act::Section`). With only one section the rail still shows
   it with its count, so "16" reads as "16 here", not "16 total".
2. **More-below peek** (B423) — when the grid has lines past the view, the bottom row fades out and a pill
   "▼ 12 more · Golf, Mods" sits over the fade (a tap scrolls one page). Same at the top ("▲ Favorites") when
   scrolled down.
3. **Footer** (B424) — "8 of 16 in Hot Shots Tennis · 28 characters" so the total is always stated.
4. **Empty-mod hint** (B425) — with no mods installed at all, **1P's rail only** ends in a ghost chip
   "+ Get more characters" that opens the Mods page's store (only where the store exists). Never in 2P–4P's columns:
   local players mustn't open the store by accident (user 2026-10-15).

All at any aspect ratio (PLAN: uniform scale, no stretching); check roster1–roster4 and roster2-favs/roster4-favs
shots at 4:3, 16:9, 21:9 and portrait. Menu screen: ours to design, no 1:1 proof needed.

## Seat cards (user 2026-10-15, mockup board "Players")
Each column is clearly its player's. **Approved, use it for sure:** the header band, a solid seat-colour strip
across the top with a big `1P`–`4P`, the input (Keyboard / Pad N), the character's name and a Near/Far side pill.
Also from the board (not yet approved separately): the seat colour on the card's border and as a tint on its
background, a faint big seat number behind the preview, the cursor tile filled in the seat colour with a 1P–4P tag
(other seats on the same tile show theirs too), and seat colours that differ in lightness, not just hue. An open
seat is dimmed with no character and a big faint seat number over "✕ to join" (B434).

# B368–B371 — per-player roster columns (user 2026-10-14)

The approved mockup is a design canvas at https://claude.ai/artifact/6kpFeK6ya6YUJ66BN9Bjzk. It has three boards: **2 players (singles)**,
**4 players (doubles)** and **Online lobby**. The user said "thats perfect" and then made three changes. This file records the final state.
The boards are 1280×720 references. Like every menu (PLAN.md), the screen is ours to design and has to lay out at any aspect ratio,
scaled uniformly, never stretched.

Why: mods add an unbounded number of characters. A single shared roster that scrolls for everyone, or one wall of faces that shrinks,
doesn't scale (both were tried in earlier mockups and rejected). Each player browses their own list instead.

## Tasks

- **B368**: the local select (`Screen::Chars`, local roster and the phone duo's) becomes one column per player: header, pills,
  preview + stats, and the grid with its own scroll. Category tabs are drawn but show one fixed list (everything).
- **B369**: category tabs, left/right overflow between them, favourites on □. This removes the disc/custom page switch (B296's □ filter)
  and the △ card toggle.
- **B370**: the online lobby (`online.rs`) with the same column for this machine's players and compact live cards for other machines'.
- **B371**: player names, Smash Bros style, on △ (after B368–B370).

## Screen layout (B368)

- Top bar: title "Choose your player" on the left. On the right, "N players · each browses on their own", then the mode pill:
  **Singles** (blue `#2f5f9d`) or **Doubles** (green `#2f7d4f`), white 2px border. The mode only follows the humans (B361); it isn't tappable.
- Below it, one column per seat in use: `repeat(n, 1fr)`, 12px gaps, 24px side margin. Singles has 2 columns (1P, 2P); doubles has 4.
  With one human, the second column is the COM.
- Background `#0e1a2e`, white text, a rounded bold face (the mockup used Baloo 2; use the menu's own font).

## A player's column

Top to bottom, inside a 14px-radius card with a 3px border in the seat colour (dashed for a COM), 10px padding, 8px gaps:

1. **Header row**, baseline-aligned: seat tag ("1P", bold 16) · the **character's name**, big (34px at 1280×720) and ellipsised if long ·
   the device on the right ("Keyboard", "Pad 1", "COM"). B371 later swaps the seat tag for the player's name.
2. **Pills row**, spread to both ends:
   - Left: costume pill with a shirt icon and the text `L1 Costume 1 R1`. L1/R1 are small button glyphs in the seat colour; use the
     legend's glyphs so a keyboard shows Q/E and a PS pad L1/R1.
   - Right: hand pill with a hand icon, "Right-handed" / "Left-handed", and the Select glyph ("Create" on PS, per legend.rs).
   - Both pills use the seat's dark "well" colour.
3. **Preview + stats row** (fixed height, ~150px at 720p):
   - The 3D preview fills the left.
   - On the right, a 150px stack: the **play style** ("All-round Player", "Baseline Player", "Net Player", "Big Server"; gold `#ffd23f`
     on the well colour) over the **pink stats card** (`#f58ac0`).
   - The card has five rows: Serve, Stroke, Volley, Impact, Footwork, as white outlined labels. Each grade is a letter in a light circle
     coloured A `#f08c00`, B `#d6336c`, C `#2f9e44`, D `#1c7ed6`, E `#9c36b5`, F `#868e96`, with 2px `#ffc2df` rules between rows.
   - Reuse the △ card's data (`main_menu.rs` "The △ attribute card", mod grades from the base character). The card and the 3D preview
     now always show together, so the `card` toggle goes.
   - Random / COM-random: "?" grades, the style hidden, "Costume ?", "Hand ?".
4. **Tabs row** (B369; B368 draws a single "All" tab): pills with the category and its count. The current tab is filled with the
   seat colour and has dark text; the others are plain.
5. **Grid**: square tiles (character face icon, 10px radius) at **~84px** at 720p, 6px gaps, fitting as many per row as the column's
   width allows. So fewer players means wider columns and more per row:
   - At 1280×720: 13 per row with 1 player, 6 with 2, 4 with 3, 3 with 4.
   - The rows that fit are shown and the rest scrolls inside this column only.
   - The selected tile has the darker seat colour and a 3px seat-colour inset outline.
   - A thin 6px scroll bar sits on the right; its thumb is (rows on view / total rows), in the seat colour.
   - Moving down past the last row scrolls the grid. Up and down never change the tab.
6. **Footer** (12px, soft seat colour): `◂ Previous-tab · 2 of 15 · Next-tab ▸` on the left, `□ favourite` on the right (B369).
   In the online lobby it is `✕ ready · □ favourite`.

Seat colours (ring / selected tile / column bg / well / tile / soft text):
- 1P red: `#ff6b6b` / `#b33b3b` / `#4a1c1c` / `#5e1f1f` / `#6a2a2a` / `#ffd0d0`
- 2P yellow: `#ffd23f` / `#8a6a1a` / `#3d300b` / `#4e3c0c` / `#5e4810` / `#fff0b8`
- 3P blue: `#5cc8ff` / `#1f6d99` / `#122d44` / `#153a57` / `#1d4a6e` / `#cdeeff`
- 4P green: `#7fd99a` / `#2f6b44` / `#15291d` / `#183022` / `#20402c` / `#c9f2d4` (dashed border for a COM)

Use the select's existing seat colours if they differ; these are the mockup's.

Input per column (each device drives only its own column; B331/B361 joining and dropping stay as they are):
- The stick or arrows move the cursor.
- L1/R1 change the costume (wrapping, locked costumes as now) and Select changes the hand.
- ✕ readies the player, ○ unreadies them and then backs out.
- △ does nothing for now (user 2026-10-14: the A–Z jump idea was dropped). B371 gives it the name picker.
- A COM column is driven by the player who picks for it, as now.
- Pointer/touch (`pointer.rs`): tap a tile to pick it, tap a tab to switch, drag or wheel to scroll a column's grid, tap the pills to
  change the costume or hand.

Done when (B368):
- `--shot`s of 1, 2, 3 and 4 players at 16:9, 4:3 and 21:9 lay out as above, with no clipping and uniform scaling.
- Each column scrolls on its own with 30+ mod characters installed.
- The legend (`legend.rs` B348 one-bar rule) lists Character, Costume (L1/R1), Hand (Select), Ready and Back, and nothing on △.
- The `legend.rs` "handled vs legend" test passes.
- `tools/check.sh -p hst` passes.

## Tabs and overflow (B369)

- Tabs in order:
  - **Favourites** (only when the player has any).
  - **Hot Shots** (the disc cast, Random first).
  - One tab per **mod game**, named by the mod's game/source (from `mod.json`; untagged mods go under "Mods"), in a fixed order.
  - Each tab shows its count.
- **No bumpers** (L1/R1 are the costume):
  - Pressing right past the last column of the grid moves to the next tab, left past the first column to the previous one, wrapping.
  - The cursor lands on the same row in the new tab, clamped, at its first or last column.
  - Up and down only scroll.
  - Tapping a tab switches to it.
- The footer shows both neighbours: `◂ Favourites · 2 of 15 · Golf ▸`.
- **□ toggles favourite** on the highlighted character. Favourites are per character (and per name once B371 lands), saved with the
  settings, and shown with a small star on the tile.
- This replaces B296's □ "next filter" and the disc/custom page. Delete that code and its tests rather than keeping both.
- Remembered per player: the last tab and cursor (B346 opens on Random; keep that for a fresh select).
- Done when: a 4-player `--shot` shows each column on a different tab; tests cover the overflow wrap, the row clamp and favourites
  persisting; the legend shows "□ Favourite".

## Online lobby (B370)

Board "Online lobby (1 local, 2 remote, 1 open)":

- **Top bar**:
  - "Online lobby".
  - A code pill: "Code" label, the join code big (e.g. `K7QX-42`), and a small QR (open it big on tap or ✕, as now).
  - The match settings in one line ("Random Court · 1 set, 4 games · Umpire: Random"; the host edits them on Court as now).
  - The mode pill.
  - The host's **Start** button ("Start ▶ 2 of 4 ready"). Guests see "Waiting for host".
- **Columns**: `2fr 1fr 1fr 1fr` with 1 local player. In general this machine's players share the wide part, and each remote or
  open seat is one narrow card.
- **This machine's players** get the full column from B368/B369. Their footer is `✕ ready · □ favourite`, and the device shows
  "You · Keyboard".
- **Other machines' players** get a compact card in their seat colour:
  - The same header: seat, the character's name smaller (24px), and the machine's name from `name_of`.
  - The same pills, without button glyphs.
  - A small preview, with the style and stats card stacked under it.
  - At the bottom, a status box: **Ready ✓** / **Picking…** and a line of context ("Your partner · Golf mod", "Browsing Hot Shots on
    their screen").
  - When they picked a mod this machine lacks, a yellow note (`#ffd23f` on dark text): "You don't have Golf: playing as its stand-in
    look" (B234/B281/M2i stand-ins).
  - Then their ping on the left and the host's action on the right ("Host: ○ remove").
  - These cards follow the remote pick **live**: costume, hand and cursor changes are sent as they happen, not only on ready.
- **Open seats** have a dashed border in the seat colour and show "COM · Lv N", "Random", "Open seat — Computer player until someone
  joins with the code". The host can edit them (✕) and remove a COM (B216's △ remove stays on the lobby screen; it isn't the
  select's △).
- Done when:
  - A native host plus a browser guest (B321 flow) show each other's picks changing live in the compact cards.
  - The mod note appears for a mod the other side lacks.
  - `--shot`s of the lobby at 16:9 and 4:3 match the board's layout.
  - The online tests pass, plus the ignored netplay runs that touch the lobby (AGENT.md list).

# B372–B373 — Browse mods as a picture grid, by kind (user 2026-10-14, top priority)

The approved concept is on the design canvas at https://claude.ai/artifact/6kpFeK6ya6YUJ66BN9Bjzk, in four boards:
**Browse mods: Characters / Courts / Umpires / Texture packs**. They are 1280×720 references. Like every menu, the screen is ours to
design and has to lay out at any aspect ratio, scaled uniformly.

Why: today Browse (`mods_screen.rs`) is a text list of 40px rows grouped under uploader headings. A mod's `preview.png` is fetched only
while that row is hovered (`modstore::preview`), so with hundreds of mods you browse names, not pictures.

- **B372** reworks Browse's page into a picture grid for **character** mods: pictures load by themselves, plus search, sort, a filter
  rail and a detail panel.
- **B373** adds the **kind tabs** (Characters · Courts · Umpires · Texture packs) across all four Mods pages, and each kind's own
  filters, tile and detail panel.

Actual store support for the other kinds isn't part of these tasks:
- Texture packs: **M5** (M5a pack kind, M5c upload, M5d download/install into the pack order).
- Courts and umpires: **M8f**.

Until those land, their tabs show an empty state that says what is coming and from which task. B373 covers M5b's "tabs at the top
of every Mods page". M5b keeps the Installed → Texture packs on/off and order list.

## Layout (B372)

- **Top bar**:
  - "Mods" on the left.
  - The page tabs between Q/E glyphs: Browse (count) · Installed (count) · Upload · My mods.
  - A **search box** (gold border, magnifier). Typing on a keyboard goes straight in. On a pad, a press opens the on-screen keyboard.
    It shows "N found" and matches name, uploader and game.
  - The **download queue pill** ("2 downloading · 1 queued", spinner; B339's queue).
- **Left: filter rail** (~190px at 720p). Groups with a gold small-caps title. Each option shows its count, and the chosen one is
  filled. For characters:
  - Game: All games, then one per `source`, then Other.
  - Plays like: Any, All-round, Baseline, Net, Big Server (from `Meta.style`).
  - Hand: Either, Left-handed (`Meta.hand`).
  - Show: Everything, Not installed, Updates, Verified only.
  - Reaching the rail: pressing left past the grid's first column moves into it; right leaves it.
- **Middle: grid**.
  - A heading line ("Get a Grip · 37 match 'ca'") with the sort options on the right: Newest · A–Z · Uploader.
  - Uploader replaces today's always-on uploader headings; B335's folding goes.
  - Tiles: 5 per row at 1280×720, the picture (aspect ~1.1), and the name under it with a small detail (hand "L"/"R").
  - The selected tile has a gold 3px inset outline and a gold name.
  - Marks on the tile:
    - a blue ✓ disc top-left when verified;
    - top-right **Installed** (green) / **Update** (gold) pills;
    - a bottom progress strip for B339/B204 downloads ("62% · downloading", "Checking…", "Queued").
  - A thin gold scroll bar on the right.
- **Right: detail panel** (300px, gold border):
  - name + "✓ Verified";
  - game · by author · version · size;
  - pills: costumes and hand;
  - the big picture, with the play style and the pink stats card beside it (the same card as the roster column in B368;
    `Meta.style`/`grades`);
  - a line of blurb ("Plays like Kent…", when uploaded);
  - **✕ Install · size** (or Update / Installed → Remove);
  - **△ More by <author>** (B255's by-author view);
  - **□ Wish list**: a saved list. Under Show, add "Wish list" as a filter.
- **Footer**:
  - left: "Pictures load by themselves: the rows on view first, then the next screen · cached for next time";
  - right: the legend (↔↕ browse · ◂ filters · ✕ install · ○ back).

## Pictures that load by themselves (B372)

- Fetch the `preview.png` of every tile on view, then of one more screen's worth in the scroll direction.
  - Run at most N fetches at once: Settings → Downloads at Once, or a separate small number if that starves installs.
  - Cancel any fetch that hasn't started once its tile scrolls far away.
- Cache on disk (desktop: beside `mods/`; browser: OPFS) under the mod's `sha256`, so an update refreshes it, and read the cache
  before the network on the next visit.
- A tile still loading shows a shimmer and the mod's initial. A mod without a picture shows its initial on a plain tile, and the
  select's own drawn picture once installed (B229/B335 path).
- **Thumbnails**: `preview.png` may be up to 4 MB (`MAX_PREVIEW`), so 20 at once is heavy.
  - Upload (`modstore::publish`) also writes a ~256px `thumb.png`, and Browse fetches the thumbnail first.
  - For entries without one, shrink the full picture once after fetching it and cache only the small one.
  - The detail panel uses the full `preview.png`.
- Done when:
  - With 100+ store mods, a Browse page fills its pictures without any hovering.
  - Scrolling fetches ahead.
  - A second visit shows them from the cache with no network.
  - The browser build does the same through OPFS.
  - A test covers which keys are on view and which are fetched ahead, and that the cache is keyed by sha256.

Pad, mouse and touch for B372:
- Tap a tile to select it, and tap again (or tap Install) to install.
- Wheel or drag scrolls.
- Tap a filter or a sort word.
- Tap the search box to type.

## Kinds (B373)

- A **kind tab row** under the page tabs, between L2/R2 glyphs (pick the keyboard keys next to the legend's). Tabs:
  **Characters · Courts · Umpires · Texture packs**, each with its count. The current one is gold.
  - A note on the right of the row says how the kind behaves online:
    - characters: a player without the mod sees a stand-in;
    - courts: a player without it plays its disc layout;
    - umpires: a missing call falls back to the disc umpire;
    - texture packs: looks only, per player, never synced to a lobby.
- The same tabs on Installed, Upload and My mods filter those lists by kind. `Meta` gets a kind field; absent means character, and
  old entries still read. The field is shared with M5c; whichever lands first adds it.
- Per kind:
  - **Courts**:
    - Rail: Game · Surface (Clay / Grass / Hard / Carpet) · Has (own crowd & NPCs, own music) · Show.
    - Tiles: 4 per row, wide (aspect ~1.45), with the court picture and its surface under the name.
    - Detail: a wide court picture; Surface; "Ball physics: same as disc court N's layout"; People; Sound; pills for day/night and
      crowd size; "Shows in the court select after installing".
  - **Umpires**:
    - Rail: Game · Calls (Full set / Some fall back) · Voice (language) · Show.
    - Tiles: 5 per row, tall (aspect ~0.9), with "34/38" calls under the name.
    - Detail: the umpire in the chair and four ▶ sample calls you can play ("Fifteen – love", "Fault!", "Deuce",
      "Game, set and match"); "34 of 38 calls — the others use the disc umpire's voice"; "Shows in the umpire select".
  - **Texture packs**:
    - Rail: Covers (Everything / Courts / Characters / Menus & HUD / Mixed) · Kind (Upscale / Recolour / New look) · Size · Show.
    - Tiles: split diagonally, disc texture on the left and the pack's on the right, labelled "disc" / "pack", with the texture count
      under the name.
    - Detail: a **before/after** picture with a divider you drag (or move with ↔); Replaces (count, what); Kind; "Online: looks only";
      "Installs: top of your pack order, switched on".
- Until M5 / M8f land, the tabs for those kinds show an empty state:
  - "Texture packs arrive with M5: until then, install packs by hand in `mods/texture-replacements/`";
  - "Courts and umpires arrive with M8f".
- Done when:
  - the four tabs switch on every Mods page by pad, keyboard, mouse and touch;
  - character filtering works on every page;
  - the legend lists L2/R2 Kind;
  - the empty states show for the kinds without store support;
  - `--shot`s at 16:9 and 4:3 lay out like the boards.

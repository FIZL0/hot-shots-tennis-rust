# B42 — court intro at match start

Split (2026-10-12). Done in B42: the camera flyover, timing and the ✕ skip (`hst_sim::cutaway::Shot::intro`,
`play/intro.rs`). Journal: `research/journal/2026-10-12-b42-court-intro/`.

What the original does (court 8 recorded, `research/b42_intro_camera.py`, `research/b42_intro_shots.py`):
- Match phase 0 (gm+0x55 = 0) is the intro. The "Now Loading" tips screen stays up first; the camera script only
  steps with the match frame counter (gm+0x58), so it holds while loading.
- Camera: `COURT/NN/CMN.XB` → `data/camed/cam_cNN_00.dat`, 40-byte records (compiler 0x381c60, shot 107 started
  by 0x382240). Courts 1, 2, 6, 10, 11 have orbit (flag 1) segments.
- Overlays during the flyover: court title plate at the top left ("Wild Green Tennis Court", surface "< Grass
  Court >" and the yellow ball rows) and "✕ Skip" at the bottom right.
- After the script (frame ~302 on court 8) the match camera shows the "Start the Match!" banner with
  "N Sets  N Games" and "Set 1" until `Rngs::intro_ticks` is up.
- Phase 0 ends when its timer, the streamed sound (0x167310) and, in doubles, the presentation flags
  0x43b1d8+0xd94/0xd95 are all done.

Sub-tasks: B42a (title plate + Skip), B42b (Start the Match banner), B42c (sound), B42d (doubles intros, modes),
B42e (online).

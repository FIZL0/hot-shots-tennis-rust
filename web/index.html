Tennis, the way it played on PS2.

OpenTennis is a from-scratch rewrite of Hot Shots Tennis. It plays the same as the original, ball for ball, and it runs in your browser. You bring the disc.

Pick your ISO Play Open source on GitHub
Play in your browser

Pick a disc image of your own copy of the game. OpenTennis copies what it needs (about 170 MB) into this browser's storage once. Nothing is uploaded, and after that you don't need the ISO.

Needs: Hot Shots Tennis, US release, SCUS-97610 v1.00, as an .iso. JP/EU discs won't work (Minna no Tennis, Everybody's Tennis).
How it works
  1. Rip your discMake an .iso of a Hot Shots Tennis disc you own (ImgBurn, dd, or your PS2 tools).
  2. Pick it hereThe game reads it on this device, in this browser. Nothing leaves your computer or phone.
  3. PlayWith a controller, keyboard or touch. It keeps working offline, and you can add it to your home screen.

To see this page again, add ?site to the address.

About

The original

Hot Shots Tennis

Clap Hanz · Sony Computer Entertainment · PlayStation 2 · 2007 (US) · SCUS-97610

The tennis game from the makers of Hot Shots Golf: big-headed characters, a simple shot system with a lot of depth, and very good computer opponents. Released in Japan as Minna no Tennis and in Europe as Everybody's Tennis.

OpenTennis is a fan project. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Sony or Clap Hanz, and it contains none of their data: everything the game shows or plays is read from your own disc while it runs.

The rewrite

OpenTennis

Rust · Bevy · open source

Written from scratch in Rust on the Bevy engine. The ball physics, shots, AI, rules and timing are ported from the original and checked against it running in an emulator, bit for bit and frame for frame. The same inputs give the same match.

The rendering is new: any resolution and aspect ratio, widescreen, upscaled textures and a high frame rate.

Online

Host a lobby and share its code or link, or join a public one. Rollback netcode keeps a match smooth over the internet; the match is the same on every screen because the game is.

Play anywhere

Controllers, keyboard, or on-screen touch controls on a phone. The browser version works offline once loaded and installs as an app.

Mods

Characters, courts, umpires

Add characters from other games, new courts and new umpires. A mod character plays on the same physics and AI as the stock cast, with its own stats.

Browse and install mods in the game (Mods › Browse), or add a .zip of your own. Players share mods through the mod store on Hugging Face; to upload, sign in under Settings & files or in the game.

Making one? The mod standard, a tutorial and the tools are in opentennis-mods.

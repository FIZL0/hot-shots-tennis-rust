# B240 — props in front of post-point cameras

Cut-away/replay cameras on Alpine Heights (and other courts) sometimes stood in a prop that then filled the lens.
The original has a mechanism for this, and it is now ported (`crates/hst/src/play/net_hide.rs`, `buried`).

# N1f — follow-through after contact (FINAL)
- Code: `hst-sim::motion` (`recovery`, `follow_over`), `crates/hst/src/play.rs` (`follow_through`, `played_out` system). Test in `crates/hst-sim/tests/motion.rs`: `anim_s05_follow_through`. Research script: `research/stroke_exit.py`.
- Player fields: +0x3f00 frames since contact (zeroed at the swing start, counted while the stroke countdown +0x3ec4 is -1); +0x3e50 recovery (30 set at the stroke start, lowered to 15 at the hit when the shot is a slice on branch 1/2; the serve branch has its own rule, not ported); +0x3e54 whiff re-press window (already ported as WHIFF_REPRESS).
- Decision once the counter reaches the recovery: stick -> run, shot press -> a new stroke, neither -> wait until the motion's sampled time >= its length -> stand. Branch 3 uses +0x3f90 = 50 instead (not ported).
- Fixture evidence: the first cut lands when the recorded counter goes 31->32 (or 16->17); played-out strokes switch the frame after sampled == length. 16 strokes play out on the exact frame, 22 are broken off (18 on the first frame allowed), every recovery matches the shot.
- App bug: after contact `human()` still called `locomote` with a zero stick, and locomote sets the stand/run motion, so the stroke was cut right after the hit; the swing state also ended 16 frames after contact. Fix: no locomotion during the swing; the swing lasts until the recovery rule above fires. In an all-bot app match the hand-offs come at 32 and 17 frames after contact, the same counters as the fixture.
- Not done (ponytail): the stand-in AI always breaks off at the recovery; serve and branch 3 rules not ported.
